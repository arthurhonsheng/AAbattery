Lots of options!

-Ridesharing apps Uber & Didi are available (Didi tends to be cheaper).

-Public Transport. The Airport express comes on a regular basis and takes you to Wellington Station. You can pay with debit/credit card, cash or Snapper card.

To & from the wedding

We basically have you sorted!

We have organised buses that will pick you up and drop you off from the designated spot which will be Te Papa, 55 Cable Street.

Te Papa is in the heart of the CBD so there will be many options to get there via ridesharing apps, public transport, and taxi.

Getting around Pōneke Wellington

There is a lot of flexibility

If you’re staying in Wellington CBD & surrounding suburbs then public transport & ride sharing apps will have you covered.

If you’re staying further out like the Hutt Valley, getting to and from Te Papa is easy enough via public transport & ride sharing it’ll just be a longer journey. Allow for 30-40 minutes.

We would recommend a car if you plan to explore outside the CBD while you’re here and want to drive to particular spots that is hard to get to via public transport.

Accommodation Information

Transport to and from the wedding venue will be organised for you, so you don’t need to find accomodation close to the wedding venue. We’d recommend booking somewhere that’s an easy and convenient commute to Te Papa.

If you’re travelling from out of town and planning to book an AirBnb, we’d suggest staying in the Wellington CBD or surrounding suburbs like Te Aro, Mount Victoria, Thorndon, Oriental Bay, Newtown, or Kelburn.
